# eSIM Activation and Transfer Policy

Document reference TC-POL-006. Issued by TeleCore Communications. Effective 1 December 2026.
This policy is published as a Markdown update and ingested through `POST /policy/ingest/text`.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers eSIM profiles issued by TeleCore Communications for phones, tablets and
smartwatches. It applies to postpaid and prepaid accounts. Physical SIM replacement is covered by
TC-POL-003 SIM Swap and Account Security Policy.

## 2. Activating a new eSIM

- A new eSIM is activated by scanning a one-time QR code in the TeleCore app or from the order
  confirmation email.
- Each QR code can be installed once and expires 30 days after it is issued.
- Activation normally completes within 15 minutes. If the line is not active after 4 hours, the
  customer may request a replacement QR code at no charge.
- The first eSIM on an account is free. Each additional eSIM profile on the same line costs
  5.00 USD.

## 3. Transferring an eSIM to a new device

- Customers can transfer an eSIM between their own devices up to 3 times in any rolling
  30-day period at no charge.
- A fourth or later transfer in the same 30-day period costs 10.00 USD per transfer.
- A transfer is a security event: before a new QR code is issued, the customer must pass the
  identity checks in TC-POL-003, and a 24-hour cooling-off period applies if the account password
  or registered email changed in the last 7 days.

## 4. Lost or stolen devices

- Report a lost or stolen device immediately. TeleCore suspends the eSIM profile within
  10 minutes of the report.
- A replacement eSIM for a lost or stolen device is free once per 12 months; further
  replacements cost 10.00 USD.

## 5. Smartwatch eSIMs

- A smartwatch eSIM shares the phone line's number and data allowance.
- The companion line fee is 10.00 USD per month and is waived on Unlimited Premium plans.
- Smartwatch eSIMs cannot roam outside Zone 1; see TC-POL-001 for roaming zones.

## 6. Customer care guidance

Care agents must not read a QR code or activation code aloud or send it by SMS. Codes are only
delivered in the TeleCore app or to the verified account email.
